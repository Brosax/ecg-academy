# Engine and practice defects found by the lesson review (2026-10-29)

Collected from the six stage reviewers (R, T, I, H, O, X). Each item says what the tracing / item does now and what it
should do. Thresholds: `docs/curriculum/criteria.md` (current values). Lessons were already corrected to describe the
tracings as they are; after a fix here, lesson captions that quote measured values may need re-syncing.

## A. Synth — rhythm and conduction timing (`src/synth/rhythm/**`, rhythm/AV catalogue groups, `truth/truth.ts`)

1. `avnrt` (typical): no pseudo-r′ in V1 (only a notch on the S upstroke). Typical AVNRT should show a pseudo-r′ in V1 and
   pseudo-S in II/III/aVF (the latter is fine).
2. `avrt` antidromic: QRS mostly negative in V5–V6 and I (contradicts Steurer criteria for pre-excited tachycardia).
3. `preexcited-af`: QRS width barely varies (≈158 ms with 1–3 narrower beats); should vary beat to beat (fusion), rate
   irregular, often very fast.
4. `atrial-flutter` clockwise variant ignores the `conductionRatio` dial (always ≈4:1).
5. `av-block-3` variant `infra-his`: `facts.escape` says "junctional 45/min" and `blockLevel: AV node`, but the tracing is a
   wide ventricular escape at 34/min — facts must match.
6. `atrial-fibrillation`: truth lists `short-qt` at normal QT values (seeds 624, 625) — derived-finding bug.
7. `atrial-fibrillation` variant `slow` ignores the `ventricularResponse` dial.
8. `torsades`: twisting weak in lead II (the strip lead the drills use) — should be visible in II and most leads.
9. `vt-monomorphic` default (170/min): almost all dissociated P waves hidden; keep the default realistic but make AV
   dissociation visible on at least some beats (or document `teaching` as the drill default).
10. `asystole`: perfectly flat line; real asystole has slight baseline drift/noise (and P-wave asystole variant has P waves).
11. No frequent-PAC scenario: `pac` gives one PAC per strip — add a frequent/multiple PAC variant.
12. `av-block-2-2to1`: does not allow the `pr` dial; when forced, PR falls ≈60 ms short while `applied` reports the request.
    Allow the dial and realise it exactly (2:1 with long PR is a teaching point).
13. `pjc`: retrograde P′ sits ≈51 ms inside the QRS though the catalogue says "just after the QRS"; make the position a
    variant (before / within / after) or match the comment.
14. `pvc` / `ventricular-bigeminy`: narrow notch at the start of every PVC in every lead that looks like a fusion or pacing
    spike — remove.
15. `pvc-patterns/multifocal` facts list only one origin.
16. `stemi-inferior:lcx` / `:posterior` facts.culprit says RCA while the tracing is an LCx pattern (see B).

## B. Synth — morphology (`src/synth/morph/**`, `heart/**`, `truth/findings.ts`, conduction/ischemia/chambers/repolarization/structural catalogue)

Conduction:
1. `rbbb` (and `av-block-2-mobitz2`'s RBBB beats): V1 is rR′ "M" with the notch above baseline; textbook complete RBBB is
   rsR′ (s dips below baseline). `irbbb`, bifascicular and trifascicular already show rsR′.
2. `ivcd` ≈125 ms looks like LBBB (V6 R-peak time 74 ms, rS V1, notched R in I/V5); only a 0.3–0.5 mm septal q differs.
   Make IVCD clearly nonspecific (preserved septal q, less prolonged R-peak time, mixed morphology).

Chambers and voltage:
3. `lae`: V1 terminal P only 0.6–0.7 mm (≥1 mm deep and ≥40 ms wide expected).
4. `lvh` / `lvh-strain`: only Sokolow-Lyon positive; Cornell ≈25 and Peguero ≈24 (patient male, 67 y). Make the classic
   exemplar meet Sokolow, Cornell (>28 men) and Peguero (≥28 men); keep a `voltage-only` variant if useful.
5. `lvh-strain` should show down-sloping ST depression + asymmetric T inversion in V5–V6/I/aVL.
6. `rvh`: axis ≈+100° (expect ≥+110°), S V5/V6 < 7 mm, R in aVR ≈1 mm; strain variant has no ST depression.
7. `copd-pattern`: limb QRS 6–7 mm, not low voltage (expected low limb voltage, P pulmonale, rightward axis, poor R).
8. `biventricular-hypertrophy`: Sokolow ≈10.6 mm (LVH voltage comes only from a deep S in V3).
9. `low-voltage` / emphysema variant: P ≈1 mm, axis +75° — not emphysema-like.
10. `electrical-alternans`: no alternation in the limb leads (only V3) — should alternate in most leads.

Ischaemia:
11. `stemi-posterior`: no isolated posterior form — I/aVL/V5/V6 rise 1.5–2.7 mm on every seed; V1 T negative where the
    posterior mirror should end upright; V3 only −0.4. Need an isolated posterior variant (max ST depression V1–V3/V4 with
    upright T, V7–V9 elevation ≥0.5 mm, lateral leads ≈ flat) and keep posterolateral as a variant.
12. `stemi-posterior:evolved`: R/S ≥ 1 only in V2; V1 stays rS.
13. `stemi-inferior:lcx` and `:posterior`: facts.culprit RCA though the tracing is LCx (II > III, I +0.8, V5–V6 elevated).
14. `stemi-high-lateral`: truth says "first diagonal" but V2 is depressed and V5–V6 ≈1.2 mm; cannot produce the D1 "South
    African flag" (ST↑ I, aVL, V2; ST↓ III/aVF).
15. `stemi-lateral`: I/aVL < 1 mm; reciprocal in V1–V2/aVR instead of II/III/aVF.
16. `stemi-anteroseptal`: V4 only 0.6–0.7 mm (fine) but V1–V3 must clearly meet thresholds; check.
17. `early-repolarization`: T/QRS ratio in V2–V4 equals `stemi-anterior-subtle` (proportionality cannot be taught); lateral
    J < 1 mm; ERP should have J-point elevation/notch ≥1 mm in inferolateral leads, and a lower T/QRS than subtle STEMI.
18. `wellens:b`: biphasic (+/−) T ≈4 mm deep instead of deep symmetric inversion in V2–V3 (type A should be biphasic).
19. `takotsubo:late`: T inversion ≤ 2 mm (V4–V6) — should be deep, widespread, with long QT.
20. `sgarbossa:smith`: V4 also meets original criterion 1; V2 ratio −0.22 — make the variant clean (Smith ratio ≥25 % in
    V2–V3, without other criteria).
21. `sgarbossa:concordant-depression`: big concordant elevation in II/aVF/V5–V6 swamps the V1–V3 concordant depression the
    variant should isolate.

Repolarisation, channelopathies, drugs, systemic:
22. `long-qt` variants: `drug` patch sets qtcMs 440 (< dial min 490), applies no dials; `lqt2`/`lqt3` apply rate not qtc;
    all three labelled sinus-bradycardia at exactly 60/min.
23. `apical-hcm`: ≥10 mm T inversion only in V4 (V5 9.7) — giant T inversion V3–V5.
24. `hcm`: no LAE (optional; fine if documented).
25. `brugada-type2`: V1 looks borderline coved; saddleback clear only in V2.
26. `athlete`: lists `early-repolarization` with J 0.1–0.2 mm.
27. `digoxin-effect`: ST sag only 0.5–0.8 mm in V4–V6, barely visible in II; QTcF ≈392 (not short).
28. `hypothermia`: Osborn wave is a ≈20 ms notch at QRS end, not a broad dome proportional to cold.
29. `na-channel-blockade`: no Brugada-type ST in V1 (optional variant).
30. `pericarditis`: PR depression < 1 mm.
31. Missing scenarios (add if feasible): bidirectional VT, AF with complete block ("regularised AF"), Spodick sign
    (pericarditis downsloping TP).

## C. Practice (`src/practice/**`)

1. `occlusion` items on `lbbb` / `rate-dependent-bbb` (#6, #7, #24, #41, #83, #111 …) answer "not an occlusion" for 40–60 min
   chest pain — contradicts ESC 2023 (BBB + ongoing ischaemic symptoms → treat as possible occlusion; apply Sgarbossa/Smith).
2. `occlusion#14` (hyperacute T alone) answers "immediate reperfusion" while i7 teaches "probable occlusion, repeat in
   15–30 min" — align with the three-way call.
3. `occlusion#16` (`stemi-anterior-subtle`) quotes STEMI thresholds the values do not meet; `#20`, `#36` describe a
   Sgarbossa tracing in plain STEMI wording; `#27` and `#70` are duplicates.
4. `territory#11` (`stemi-inferior:lcx`) says "III > II suggests the RCA"; `stemi-posterior` explanations claim an upright
   T in V1–V3; `territory#1`, `#30` ask for the territory on an LBBB tracing.
5. `triage` drill: pre-excited AF graded "review today" (should be act now: `preexcited-af` findings atrial-fibrillation +
   wpw are both major — add an override); any `long-qt` graded act now regardless of QTc (act now only ≥500 ms or with
   torsades/symptoms).
6. `narrow-tachy` family includes wide/irregular items (antidromic AVRT seeds 3/24/29, pre-excited AF 6, MAT, AF); its
   AVNRT explanation uses typical text for atypical and "RP′ < 70 ms" (criteria.md says 90 ms).
7. `wide-tachy` has no SVT-with-aberrancy answer, so it cannot test VT vs aberrancy.
8. `irregular` 2/22/30 are `paced-vvi` items keyed as AF; `rate-atrial` almost all AV blocks (only seed 9 is flutter);
   `rate-irregular:20` AF item truth 37 bpm with dial 75.
9. `ectopy` on `pac-aberrant` says the QRS is "like the sinus ones" (it is wide); `p-qrs` on AV dissociation without block
   says "e.g. complete AV block"; `brady` answers "junctional escape rhythm" for complete AV block scenarios.
10. `chambers`: partial-IAB items offer LAE as a distractor although definitions overlap; LAE explanation claims a "deep" V1
    terminal P; Sokolow written "≥35 mm" (criteria: >35 mm / >3.5 mV).
11. `substrates`: ERP explanation requires "concave ST elevation" (not required); substrates#5 (takotsubo late) keyed as
    "Prolonged QT".
12. `metabolic`, `systemic`, `artefacts` choose the topic from the seed — add an optional topic filter so lessons and drills
    can request a topic instead of hunting seeds.
13. Real data: case 18159 has `AFIB: 0` (zero confidence) but maps to `atrial-fibrillation` — mapping should ignore 0-likelihood
    diagnostic codes where PTB-XL semantics require (rhythm codes legitimately carry 0).
